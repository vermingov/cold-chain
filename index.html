Cold Chain

An unfinished game. The sandbox is the game now; the first night of the story is still here, and so are the rooms it was built from.

Play: the sandbox
  • A concrete hall with people in it, and knives, a sword, a bat, a pistol, a rifle and a shotgun.
  • Everyone has a real skeleton and organs inside them. Wounds bleed by what they hit, and the readings on the right are theirs.
  • Left click to use what you hold, right click to cut with a blade. 1 to 8 change it. N adds a person, X shows inside them.
The story: Thursday night
  • The phone call, the grave, the cold room, the handover at the gate, and home.
  • Press E to use whatever you are looking at. Tab holds up your clipboard.
The yard
  • Click to capture the mouse. W A S D to walk.
  • Hold Shift to run until you are out of breath. Hold Ctrl or C to crouch.
  • Get close to a headstone with the torch on it to read it.
The table
  • Press 1 for the scalpel. Hold the left mouse button and drag to cut. The slower you drag, the deeper it goes.
  • Go over the same line with slow strokes to get through the belly wall. Too slow and you cut into what is underneath.
  • Press 2 for your free hand. Hold the left mouse button on the edge of the cut and drag to pull it open, then grab what is inside. The mouse wheel lifts what you are holding.
  • Press 3 for a retractor. Hold the left mouse button on an edge of the cut, drag it back and let go, and it stays there. Click it again to take it out.
