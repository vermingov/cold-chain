Cold Chain

An unfinished game. The first night can be played through; the rooms are below on their own as well.

Play: Thursday night
  • The call, the grave on the order, the cold room, the gate, home.
  • E uses what is in front of you. Tab holds up the clipboard.
The yard
  • Click to take the mouse. W A S D to walk.
  • Shift runs, until the breath goes. Ctrl gets down.
  • Hold the torch close to a stone to read it.
The table
  • 1 is the scalpel. Hold the button and draw. Slow is deep.
  • Slow strokes, again and again down the same line, go through the wall. Too slow goes into what is under.
  • 2 is the bare hand. Draw the lip of the wound to pull it open, then take hold of what is inside.
  • 3 is a retractor. Hook a lip, draw it back, let go: it stays. Hook it again to take it out.
